# Microsoft Outlook

Give agents the context they need from your Outlook mailbox.

- Search mail with Outlook's search syntax or filters, and read whole conversations.
- Download attachments to work with alongside your other files.
- With **Read and draft** access: prepare drafts, replies and forwards (with
  attachments) for you to review and send from Outlook.
- With **Read and write** access: also send, reply and forward, mark messages
  read or unread, flag and categorize them, and organize mail into folders.

Works with Outlook.com and Microsoft 365 work or school mailboxes. Read-only
access cannot send mail or change your mailbox. Read and draft access can
create, update and delete drafts, including ones you wrote, but Houston
refuses every request to send. Deleted messages go to Deleted Items.
