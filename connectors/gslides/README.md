# Google Slides

Let agents read your decks, check how each slide looks and build new ones.

- Read every slide's text and speaker notes, and the presentation's comments.
- See a presentation's layout and structure, and preview any slide as an
  image.
- With **Read and write** access: create presentations, add, reorder, copy and
  delete slides, write and replace text and notes, add shapes, images and
  tables, format text, and comment and reply.

Agents work with a presentation when you share its link. To let them search
your Drive for presentations, also connect Google Drive. Read-only access
cannot change your presentations.
