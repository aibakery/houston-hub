# Pennylane

Connect your Pennylane company so your agents can answer finance questions: which
customer invoices are unpaid or overdue, what a supplier billed this quarter, which
bank transactions are still unmatched, and how your trial balance, journals and
ledger entries stand. They can also read quotes, recurring invoices, products,
analytical categories and payment mandates, follow recent changes, and prepare FEC
or general ledger exports for a period.

With read and write access, agents can also create and send invoices and quotes,
add customers, suppliers and products, upload receipts and supplier
invoices, match transactions to invoices, categorize records and post ledger
entries. Sending a document emails your customer, and a finalized invoice can no
longer be deleted.

The connection uses an API token for one Pennylane company, limited to the
permissions you choose when you create it.
