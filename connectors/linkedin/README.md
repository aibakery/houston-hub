# LinkedIn

Let agents share updates on LinkedIn as you: announce a launch, share an article
with a link preview, or post a photo with alt text for accessibility. Agents can
also remove a post they published by mistake.

With read-only access, agents can only see your basic profile: your name,
profile picture and email address. Choose read and write access to let them
publish and delete posts. Posts can be public or limited to your connections.

LinkedIn does not let connected apps read your feed, existing posts, connections
or messages, so agents cannot browse or reply to LinkedIn content.

Connect with LinkedIn in one click: sign in and allow access. Your
authorization stays on Houston's server. See [Connect your LinkedIn account](SETUP.md).
