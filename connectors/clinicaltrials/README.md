# ClinicalTrials.gov

Let agents search the U.S. National Library of Medicine's public registry of
more than 600,000 clinical studies from around the world, and read each study's
record.

## Find studies

- Search by condition or disease, treatment, location, sponsor, title or
  outcome.
- Narrow results by recruitment status, phase, study type, distance from a
  place, dates and other study details.
- Count matching studies, or sort them by last update, start date or
  enrollment.

## Read study records

- Read a study's summary, design, eligibility criteria, arms and interventions,
  outcome measures, sponsors, contacts and locations.
- Read posted results: participant flow, baseline characteristics, outcome
  measures and adverse events.

Access is read-only and needs no account. The registry is free to use; its data
refreshes on weekdays. When you share results, credit "Source: ClinicalTrials.gov,
U.S. National Library of Medicine". Using the data means accepting the
[ClinicalTrials.gov terms](https://clinicaltrials.gov/about-site/terms-conditions).
This connector is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Library of
Medicine.
