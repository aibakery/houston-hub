# PubMed

Let agents search biomedical literature, read abstracts, authors and MeSH terms,
follow citations and retrieve eligible PMC full text. Access is read-only and
requires no account; an optional NCBI key provides a higher quota.

> Abstracts may be copyrighted and PMC articles have individual licences. Follow
> the rights holder's terms and [NCBI's notice](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/About/disclaimer.html).

This connector is not affiliated with the National Library of Medicine or NCBI.
