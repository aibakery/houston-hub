# PubMed

Let agents search PubMed, the U.S. National Library of Medicine's database of
more than 40 million citations for biomedical and life sciences literature, and
read open-access articles from PubMed Central (PMC).

## Find articles

- Search with the same query syntax as the PubMed website: keywords, authors,
  journals, MeSH terms, publication types, affiliations and dates.
- Sort by relevance, publication date, first author or journal, and count the
  matches.

## Read articles

- Read titles, journals, publication dates, DOIs and other identifiers.
- Read abstracts, full author lists with affiliations and ORCID iDs, MeSH terms,
  keywords, publication types, substances and grants.
- Follow citations: the articles that cite a paper, its references and similar
  articles.
- Read the full text of open-access PMC articles, section by section.

Access is read-only and needs no account. An optional free NCBI API key lets
Houston make more requests per second. Abstracts in PubMed may include material
protected by copyright, and PMC articles carry their own licenses: follow the
copyright holder's terms when you reuse them, as explained in NCBI's
[disclaimer and copyright notice](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/About/disclaimer.html).
This connector is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Library of
Medicine or NCBI.
