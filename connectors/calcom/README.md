# Cal.com

Connect your Cal.com account so your agents can see upcoming and past bookings,
find open times on your booking pages, check your calendars for busy times and
read your event types, availability schedules and workflows. They can also look
at the bookings, event types and schedules of teams you belong to.

With read and write access, agents can also book meetings, reschedule or cancel
them, confirm or decline booking requests, add guests, record no-shows and set
out-of-office time. They can create and edit event types, booking questions,
private links, availability schedules, webhooks and workflows. Bookings and their
changes email the people involved and update connected calendars. Webhooks are
visible only with read and write access, because they include signing secrets.

The connection uses a Cal.com API key from your own account, so it can reach
everything your account can. Your key stays on Houston's server.
