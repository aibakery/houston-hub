# Figma

Connect Figma so your agents can read your design files: their pages, frames
and layers, version history, team projects, published components and styles,
and comment threads. Agents can also export frames or layers as PNG, JPG, SVG or
PDF images to work with them.

With read and write access, agents can also post comments, reply to them, add
reactions and delete their own comments. Figma notifies the people following
those threads.

Figma limits how often files can be read and exported, and the limits are much
lower for View and Collab seats and the Starter plan. Your credentials stay on
Houston's server.
