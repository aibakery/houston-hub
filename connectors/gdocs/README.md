# Google Docs

Let agents read your documents, summarize them and keep them up to date.

- Read a document's text, tab by tab, including its tables.
- See its structure and formatting, its comments and the suggestions in
  progress.
- With **Read and write** access: create documents, add and replace text,
  insert tables, images and page breaks, apply headings and styles, add tabs,
  comment and reply, suggest edits for you to review, and accept or reject
  suggestions.

Agents work with a document when you share its link. To let them search your
Drive for documents, also connect Google Drive. Read-only access cannot change
your documents.
