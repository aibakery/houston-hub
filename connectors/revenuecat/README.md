# RevenueCat

Connect RevenueCat so your agents can answer subscription questions: how your
MRR, active subscriptions and trials stand today, whether a customer's
subscription is active, in a trial or in billing retry, what they bought and
which entitlements they have, and how your products, entitlements, offerings and
packages are set up across your apps.

With read and write access, agents can also grant a customer promotional access
to an entitlement until a chosen date or revoke it, and edit your offerings:
create them, rename them, make one current, and add, reorder or remove their
packages and products. Changes reach your apps the next time they load their
offerings.

Customer lists show only when and on which platform each customer was seen;
agents open a single customer for more, and read personal attributes such as an
email address only when they need them. Your API key's permissions still apply,
and the key stays on Houston's server.
