# Google Forms

Let agents collect what people told you and turn it into summaries, follow-ups
and reports.

- Read a form's questions and settings, and every response submitted to it.
- Check for new responses since the last time an agent looked.
- With **Read and write** access: create forms, add, edit, reorder and remove
  questions, turn forms into quizzes, and open or close them to new responses.

Agents work with a form when you share its edit link. To let them search your
Drive for forms, also connect Google Drive. Responses cannot be changed or
deleted, and read-only access cannot change your forms.
