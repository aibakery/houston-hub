# Microsoft Outlook Calendar

Let agents see what is on your Outlook calendar and help plan your time.

- Review upcoming events across your calendars, including recurring ones.
- Find free time for you and your colleagues (work or school accounts).
- With **Read and write** access: create, update and cancel events, invite
  attendees, add Teams links, and accept, tentatively accept or decline
  invitations.

Works with Outlook.com and Microsoft 365 work or school calendars. Read-only
access cannot change your calendars or notify anyone. With Read and write
access, Outlook emails attendees when you create, change or cancel a meeting
you organize, and the organizer when you reply to an invitation.
