# Carta CRM

Connect Carta CRM so your agents can review your deal pipeline and its stage
history, follow fundraising prospects and investors, look up companies and
contacts with your team's past emails and meetings, find who can introduce you,
read notes, tasks, fees, investment committee meetings and saved reports, and
search Carta's catalogue of institutional LPs.

With read and write access, agents can also create and update companies,
contacts, deals, fundraising prospects, investors, fees, notes, tasks and
investment committee items, add records to lists and themes, link notes, and
delete records. Deletions are permanent, except for fees.

Each area needs its module enabled in your Carta CRM organization, and Fund Admin
details need Fund Admin access. Houston never sends emails to LPs, changes your
organization's users or settings, or reshapes your custom fields and stages. Your
API key stays on Houston's server.
