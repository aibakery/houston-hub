# Resend

Connect your Resend account so your agents can follow the email your product
sends: what went out, whether it was delivered, bounced, opened or clicked, and
delivery statistics over time. They can read email received on your domains,
including attachments, search the threads of your Resend inboxes, and look up
contacts, segments, topics, broadcasts, templates, automations, domains,
webhooks, suppressed addresses, recent API activity and your plan usage.

With read and write access, agents can also send email, one message or up to 100
at a time, schedule or cancel it, and run broadcasts to a segment of your
contacts. In inboxes, they can reply, forward, write and send drafts, label
threads, mark them read and file them. They can manage contacts and their
subscriptions, import contacts from a CSV file, and create and publish
templates. They can also manage domains, webhooks, automations, suppressions and
inboxes. Sends, replies, broadcasts and automations reach real people, and your
Resend plan's sending limits apply.

The connection uses a Resend API key from your team, which stays on Houston's
server.
