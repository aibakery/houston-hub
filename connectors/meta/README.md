# Meta Business Suite

Connect one Facebook Page, and the Instagram professional account linked to it, so
your agents can work with what you see in Meta Business Suite. They can read your
posts, reels and stories with their comments and reactions, see scheduled posts,
tags and reviews, read Messenger and Instagram Direct conversations, and pull Page,
post, account and media insights such as views, reach, follows and engagement.

With read and write access, agents can also publish and schedule Facebook posts,
photos and videos, publish Instagram photos, reels, stories and carousels from
images and videos already online, reply to and hide or delete comments, and answer
Messenger and Instagram messages as your business. Meta only lets businesses reply
to messages within 24 hours of a person's last message.

Each connection serves one Page. Ads and publishing Facebook reels are not
included. You need a Meta business portfolio that manages the Page and a free Meta
developer app. Posts, comments and insights need no App Review; conversations with
your customers need Meta's Advanced Access for messaging, which requires business
verification and App Review. Your access token stays on Houston's server.
