# Google Calendar

Let agents see what is on your calendar and help plan your time.

- Review upcoming events across your calendars, including recurring ones.
- Find free time with busy/free availability.
- With **Read and write** access: create, update, move and cancel events,
  invite attendees and add Google Meet links.

Read-only access cannot change your calendars or notify attendees.
