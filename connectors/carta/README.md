# Carta

Connect Carta so your agents can answer equity questions from the source. For
companies, they can read cap tables by share class or stakeholder, stakeholders and
their equity, option grants, RSUs, RSAs, certificates, convertible notes, exercises,
transaction history, share classes, vesting templates and 409A valuations. For
investment firms, they can read funds, investments and their cap tables, holdings,
cash balances, performance metrics, partners and their contacts. For shareholders,
they can read portfolios, holdings, current fair market values, transactions and fund
documents shared with limited partners.

With read and write access, agents can also book capital calls, management fees and
carried interest allocations, draft option grants, record exercise payments and tax
withholding, settle secondary transactions, start companies on Carta Launch and
upload Open Cap Table files. Fund accounting entries are recorded once even if a
request is repeated.

When you sign in, Carta asks which companies, firms or portfolios to share, and your
Carta role still limits what agents can see. Your credentials stay on Houston's server.
