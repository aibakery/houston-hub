# Exa

Let agents search the web with [Exa](https://exa.ai), a search engine built for
AI, and read what they find.

## Search the web

- Search in plain language and narrow results to chosen sites, publication
  dates, a country, or categories such as news, research publications,
  companies, people and financial reports.
- Choose quick, low-cost searches or deeper multi-step research that writes a
  cited synthesis of the results.
- Find pages similar to a page you already have.

## Read pages

- Read search results or any web page as clean text, as the passages most
  relevant to a question, or as a short AI summary.
- Ask for fresh copies of pages that change often, or a page as it was on a past
  date.
- Get a direct answer to a question, with citations to its sources.

Access is read-only: the connector searches and reads, and changes nothing in
your Exa account or on the web. It does not run Exa Agent research tasks,
monitors, batches or Websets.

Connecting requires an Exa API key. Exa charges every search, page read and
answer to your Exa account's credits, and new accounts include free monthly
credits. To keep costs and answers predictable, Houston limits how many
results and how much page text each request can return. Your key stays on
Houston's server.
