# ChEMBL

Let agents search EMBL-EBI's ChEMBL, an open database of more than two million
drug-like molecules, the proteins and other targets they act on, and the
bioactivity measurements, assays and publications behind them.

## Find molecules

- Look up a compound or drug by name, trade name, ChEMBL ID or structure.
- Find similar molecules, or molecules that contain a chemical substructure.
- Narrow results by development phase, approval year, molecular weight,
  lipophilicity, drug-likeness and other properties.

## Explore biology and drugs

- Search targets by name, organism or UniProt accession.
- Read bioactivity measurements such as IC50 or Ki for a compound, a target or
  an assay, and compare potencies.
- Read the assays and publications the data comes from.
- Read curated drug mechanisms of action and the diseases drugs are approved or
  studied for.
