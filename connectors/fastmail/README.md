# Fastmail

Connect your mailbox to Houston with a Fastmail JMAP API token. Search conversations,
read messages, and download attachments without moving your mail out of Fastmail.

## Read and search

- Browse folders, messages, and conversation threads.
- Search by sender, recipient, subject, message text, date, folder, or alias.
- Download attachments into your session.
- List sending identities and masked email addresses.

## Optional read-write access

Choose **Read and write** during setup to send messages, reply to a sender or an
entire thread, and organize your mailbox. You can choose a From alias and attach
files from your session.

You can also create folders, move or archive messages, and move mail to Trash.
The `destroyMessage` function permanently deletes messages.

## What you need

A Fastmail plan that supports API tokens (above Basic), and a **JMAP** token with
**Email** enabled. Sending requires **Email submission**; listing masked addresses
requires **Masked Email**.

See the setup instructions below to connect your account.
