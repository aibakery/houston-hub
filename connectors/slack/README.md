# Slack

Let agents catch you up on Slack: summarize a busy channel, read the thread where
a decision was made, or pull an attachment someone shared. They see the channels,
private channels and direct messages you can see, and link their answers back to
the original messages.

With read and write access, agents can also act as you: post or reply in a
thread, send a direct message, edit or delete your messages, react with an
emoji, or share a file. Everything they post appears under your name.

Read-only access lets agents read, but never post. Each connection covers one
Slack workspace and is personal: only you can use it. Add another connection for
each additional workspace.

Connecting Slack also adds Houston to Slackbot. Open a direct message with
Slackbot, select **Apps**, add Houston and sign in. Slackbot can then use your
Houston connections and saved workflows, and asks you before each action.

Your agent's summaries and answers may be inaccurate or incomplete. Check
important details in Slack before you rely on them.

Add to Slack in one click: pick your workspace and allow access. Your
authorization stays on Houston's server. See
[Connect your Slack account](SETUP.md).
