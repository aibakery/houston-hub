# Wise

Connect your Wise business account so your agents can check balances in every
currency, review account activity and transfers, look up exchange rates, read
your receiving bank details and save transfer receipts.

With read and write access, agents can also price transfers, add recipients,
draft transfers and payment batches, open or close balances, and move money
between your balances and jars. Wise still decides what your token may do.

Wise lets personal API tokens fund transfers and export balance statements only
for accounts based in the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore and
Malaysia. Elsewhere, agents can prepare a transfer, and you approve and pay it
in the Wise app or website.

Each connection uses either Wise's live service or its sandbox, which is useful
for trying payments without real money. Card details, webhooks and profile
changes are not available through this connector.
