# HubSpot

Find and read your HubSpot contacts, companies, deals and tickets, see how they are
linked to each other, and follow their notes, tasks, calls, meetings and logged
emails. Agents can also look up deal and ticket pipelines and their stages, the
account's owners, and the properties each kind of record has.

Read-only is the default. Read and write adds creating and updating records, linking
and unlinking them, logging notes and tasks, and moving deals between stages.

> The connection sees the whole HubSpot account, not only the records of the person
> who connected it. HubSpot's permissions for the connected app or key still apply.

Connect by signing in with HubSpot, or with an access token. Deleting records, bulk
imports, custom objects, marketing tools and files are not available, and HubSpot's
request limits apply.
