# incident.io

Connect incident.io so your agents can follow your incidents: what is happening
now, who leads each incident and who else holds a role, the updates shared so
far, its timeline and key timestamps, and the follow-ups that came out of it.
They can also look up your alerts, see who is on call on each schedule now or
next week, and read your catalog of services, teams and other entries.

With read and write access, agents can also declare incidents, change an
incident's name, summary, severity, status, roles, timestamps or custom fields,
share updates, and create follow-ups. Declaring an incident is real: incident.io
announces it, opens its channel and runs your workflows, which can page the
people on call. Agents declare one only when you ask.

The API key's own permissions still apply, and it stays on Houston's server.
