# lemlist

Connect your lemlist team so your agents can review campaigns and their
statistics, see where each lead stands, read inbox conversations, check tasks
and activity, and look up contacts, companies, signal agents and the people
database. They can also read sequences, schedules, senders, templates,
unsubscribes and deliverability alerts.

With write access, agents can also create and edit campaigns and their
sequences, add, pause or remove leads, mark them interested, send emails and
LinkedIn, WhatsApp or SMS messages, manage tasks, contacts, companies and lists,
unsubscribe people, run enrichments and contact sourcing, and manage signal
agents, webhooks and lemwarm. Messages reach real people as soon as they are
sent, enrichments spend your lemlist credits, and merges and deletions cannot be
undone. Team settings are visible only with write access, because they include
webhook secrets.

The connection uses a lemlist API key, so it can reach everything your team can.
Your key stays on Houston's server. Connecting mailboxes, uploading voice
messages and CSV exports stay in lemlist.
