# Vercel

Connect Vercel so your agents can check your projects and their deployments,
follow a build's status and logs, read recent runtime logs of a deployment, and
review your domains and whether their DNS is set up correctly.

With read and write access, agents can also redeploy, promote a deployment to
production or roll production back, cancel a build, and read and manage your
projects' environment variables. Environment variables are only available with
read and write access, because their values are often secrets.

Vercel still applies your token's scope and your team's plan. Your credentials
stay on Houston's server.
