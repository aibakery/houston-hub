# Microsoft OneDrive

Help agents find the files and documents you need without hunting through folders.

- Search your OneDrive by name or content, browse folders, and check who an item
  is shared with.
- Download files, or convert Word, Excel and PowerPoint documents to PDF.
- With **Read and write** access: upload files up to 250 MB, create folders,
  rename, move, copy and delete items, and share them with people or by link.

Works with personal Microsoft accounts and work or school accounts. Read-only
access cannot change, share or delete your files, and deleted items go to the
OneDrive recycle bin.
