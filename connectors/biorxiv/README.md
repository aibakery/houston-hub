# bioRxiv and medRxiv

Let agents find and read preprints from bioRxiv, the preprint server for
biology, and medRxiv, the preprint server for health sciences. Preprints are
complete research papers shared before peer review.

## Find preprints

- Search titles, abstracts, authors and other fields of both servers, or one of
  them, and sort by relevance, date or citations.
- List everything posted on a server during a period, optionally limited to a
  subject category such as neuroscience or epidemiology, or to a funder.
- List the preprints that were published in a journal during a period.

## Read preprints

- Read every version of a preprint: title, authors, corresponding author and
  institution, posting date, category, license and abstract.
- See whether a preprint became a journal article, with its journal, date and
  DOI, or find the preprint behind a journal article.
- Save a preprint's PDF to a session file, and get its address to open in a
  browser.

Access is read-only and needs no account. Search is powered by
[Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org), run by EMBL-EBI, which indexes both servers
and is usually current to the previous day; everything else comes from the
[bioRxiv and medRxiv API](https://api.biorxiv.org). Both websites limit
automated PDF downloads: a download can be refused, and medRxiv currently blocks
them, so people can open the PDF in their browser instead. Each preprint carries
its authors' chosen license; follow it when you reuse the work, and credit
bioRxiv, medRxiv and Europe PMC as described in
[Europe PMC's terms of use](https://europepmc.org/Copyright). This connector is
not affiliated with or endorsed by bioRxiv, medRxiv, Cold Spring Harbor
Laboratory or EMBL-EBI.
