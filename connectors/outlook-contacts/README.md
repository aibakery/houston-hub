# Microsoft Outlook Contacts

Let agents find the people in your Outlook address book and keep it tidy.

- Look up contacts by email address and read their phone numbers, addresses,
  companies and notes.
- Browse your contact folders, including nested ones.
- With **Read and write** access: add, update and delete contacts, and create,
  rename, move and delete contact folders.

Works with Outlook.com and Microsoft 365 work or school accounts. It reaches
only your own contacts, not your organization's directory or contacts others
share with you. Deleted contacts and folders go to Deleted Items, and changes
notify nobody.
